The Tilly idea

A room is part of the ritual.

Tilly House was imagined as more than a place to sleep and more than a place to drink matcha. Upstairs, AOARASHI and CHITOSE create two different rhythms for resting. Downstairs, the café keeps the stay connected to the smell, texture and pace of a freshly prepared cup.

The result is intentionally small: two rooms, one café and one address on Pasteur — designed for the kind of pause that fits the time you actually have.

The stay story

Brewed for Moment.
Made for Staying.

The rooms were named to express two different feelings rather than two different levels of hospitality. AOARASHI — “green wind” — is compact and light. CHITOSE — “timeless” — gives the guest more room to spread out and stay with the moment.

Both sit above the same café, so a matcha, Houjicha or coffee can become part of the room instead of a separate stop on the day.

A small house, intentionally

Not a hotel.
Not only a café.

Upstairs are just two rooms: AOARASHI and CHITOSE. Downstairs is a matcha, coffee & tea house. Keeping the scale small lets the experience stay clear — arrive easily, settle in, order a cup and use the room for exactly the amount of time you need.

Japanese-inspired restraint guides the mood rather than becoming a theme: quieter materials, warm neutral light, clean lines, thoughtful details and enough space for the guest to slow the day down.

AOARASHI青嵐 — “green wind.” A compact 25 m² room for lighter, shorter pauses.
CHITOSE千歳 — “timeless.” A larger 45 m² room with more room to settle in.
The caféMatcha, Houjicha, coffee, tea, cacao, pastries and a digital ordering flow connected to the rooms.
The city46E Pasteur keeps Tilly House close to central Saigon while the interior creates a quieter pace.

From check-in to first sip

Hospitality that keeps moving with you.

A guest can arrive for a two-hour reset, spend eight hours working and resting, or stay overnight. The café is not treated as a separate business inside the website: it follows the guest journey, including ordering to AOARASHI or CHITOSE.

That connection is what makes Tilly House different. The stay gives the drink a place to belong; the café gives the room another reason to feel lived in.
